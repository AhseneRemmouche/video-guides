# Membership

## Who can join
Anyone who lives, works or studies in Harbor City can get a library card for free. Visitors can buy a temporary card for 10 EUR, valid for 3 months.

## How to join
Bring a photo ID and a proof of address (a bill or a lease less than 3 months old) to any branch. Children under 14 need a parent or guardian to sign the form.

## Card types
A standard card lets you borrow up to 12 items at a time. A junior card (under 14) lets you borrow up to 8 items. A temporary card lets you borrow up to 4 items.
