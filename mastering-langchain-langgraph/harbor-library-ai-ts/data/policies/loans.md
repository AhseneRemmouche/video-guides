# Loans and renewals

## Loan periods
Books are loaned for 21 days. DVDs and video games are loaned for 7 days. Magazines are loaned for 7 days, except the latest issue, which stays in the library.

## Renewals
You can renew an item twice, online or at any branch, as long as nobody has placed a hold on it. Each renewal adds a new full loan period from the day you renew.

## Holds
You can place a hold on any item that is checked out. When it comes back, we keep it for you for 7 days at the branch you chose. You can have at most 5 active holds.
