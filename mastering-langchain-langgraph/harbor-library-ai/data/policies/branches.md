# Branches and opening hours

## Central Library
Address: 1 Quay Street. Open Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 8 pm, and Sunday, 1 pm to 6 pm. The Central Library has the music collection, the local history archive and the maker space.

## Northside Branch
Address: 48 Mill Road. Open Tuesday to Saturday, 10 am to 6 pm. Closed on Sunday and Monday.

## Lighthouse Branch
Address: 7 Beacon Square. Open Monday to Friday, 12 pm to 7 pm. Closed at weekends. The Lighthouse Branch specializes in children's books and holds story time every Wednesday at 4 pm.
