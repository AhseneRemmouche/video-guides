# Late fees and lost items

## Late fees
Adult cards pay 0.20 EUR per item per day late, up to a maximum of 5 EUR per item. Junior cards never pay late fees.

## Blocked cards
If you owe 15 EUR or more, your card is blocked for borrowing until you pay. You can still use the computers and the reading rooms.

## Lost or damaged items
If you lose or damage an item, you pay its replacement price plus a 3 EUR processing fee. If you find a lost item within 6 months and return it in good condition, we refund the replacement price but not the processing fee.
