# Study rooms and the maker space

## Study rooms
The Central Library and the Northside Branch have study rooms for 2 to 6 people. Members can book a room for up to 3 hours per day, up to 14 days in advance. A booking is cancelled if you are more than 15 minutes late.

## Maker space
The maker space at the Central Library has two 3D printers, a laser cutter and sewing machines. You must attend a free 1-hour safety session before you use the laser cutter. 3D printing costs 0.05 EUR per gram of material.
