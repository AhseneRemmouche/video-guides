# Digital services

## E-books and audiobooks
Members can borrow up to 5 e-books and 5 audiobooks at a time through the HarborReads app. Digital loans last 14 days and return themselves automatically, so there are never late fees on digital items.

## Wi-Fi and computers
Free Wi-Fi is available in all branches; the network is called HarborLibrary-Guest and needs no password. Members can book a computer for up to 2 hours per day.

## Printing
Printing costs 0.10 EUR per black-and-white page and 0.50 EUR per color page. You pay at the self-service kiosk with your card.
