Socialix documentation

Socialix is a dashboard where a team of five Claude agents researches, writes and schedules social media posts for you. Approved posts are published by your WordPress Social Autopilot, which already holds your connected social accounts.

How the pieces fit together

 You ──► Socialix dashboard (http://localhost:3100)
            │  Master Agent ─► Research ─► Creator ─► Scheduler      (Claude, via your Anthropic key)
            │
            │  posts wait for your Approve
            ▼
         Social Publisher MCP server (inside Socialix)
            │  puts the approved post in the autopilot's publishing queue
            ▼
         WordPress Social Autopilot database (Docker container wsa-pg)
            │
            ▼
         Autopilot worker (npm run worker) ─► LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Facebook...

Three programs are involved:

  1. The database (Docker container wsa-pg). Both apps store their data in it.
  2. Socialix (this project). It plans and writes posts and waits for your approval.
  3. The autopilot worker. It actually publishes. If it is not running, approved posts wait in the queue and go out when it starts.

Safety in one paragraph

Nothing is published until you press Approve on a post, unless you switch on auto-publish in Settings. Socialix never sees your social media passwords or tokens: they stay in the autopilot. The Anthropic API key is read from the autopilot's .env file and never copied.
