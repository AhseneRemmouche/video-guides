Our Socialix, page by page

Overview (step 09)

Like the video, the Overview greets you with "Your AI creative team is on it." It shows the current campaign and how many of its posts are published, three counters (scheduled, published, connected accounts "through the autopilot"), the office picture, upcoming posts and a "Recent activity" list of finished tasks. The "Brief the team" button opens the AI Agent page.

New campaign (step 07)

Our campaign form asks for a brief rather than a logo and office style: campaign name, niche or topic, audience, goal, tone of voice, the link posts should point to, and which platforms to use. The agents read this brief before every task, so a good brief gives better posts. The same fields appear under Settings (see below).

AI Agent (steps 11 to 14)

Five agent cards sit at the top (Master, Research, Creator, Scheduler, Analytics), each with a status such as Idle or Done. Click a card to chat with that agent. The Master Agent has two tabs, Chat and Tasks. While an agent works, its desk lights up in the office picture next to the chat.

This is our version of the video's task board. Each row is a task the Master Agent gave to a team member, with its status and the team member's written result.

Here is the request we used in our own test run. It shows the kind of instruction the Master Agent handles well: what to research, what to write, for which networks, and when.

Research 2 trending topics about AI tools for small businesses this week, then write one LinkedIn post and one X post about the best one, and schedule them for tomorrow at good times.

Posts (steps 17 and 18)

Posts are filtered by status: All, Awaiting approval, Drafts, Queued, Published, Failed. A post card shows the network, the text, the hashtags and the time. Approve hands the post to the autopilot's queue; Cancel takes back a queued post; Delete removes it. "Refresh statuses" asks the autopilot what happened to queued posts. At the bottom, "Create a post yourself" lets you write a post without any agent: network, title, text, hashtags, an image URL (needed for Instagram and TikTok) and an optional publish time.

Analytics (step 20)

Where the video shows views and engagement, our Analytics page shows what our app can actually know: how many posts are in each status, how many went live per platform, and the Claude usage of each agent (calls, input and output tokens, web searches). The note under the table says it plainly: likes and reach are not collected, because the autopilot publishes posts but does not read their metrics.

Accounts and Settings (steps 18 and 20)

Each network shows its status in the autopilot and its rules: the character limit, "needs an image" for Instagram and TikTok, "manual posting only" for YouTube. At test time LinkedIn, X and Instagram were connected, Facebook's token had expired, and TikTok and YouTube were disconnected.

There is no API key field. The Claude key, the database and the site address are read from the autopilot's settings file (step 04 explains why).

What you should see once the guide is done: the same seven sidebar pages as in the screenshots, five agent cards on the AI Agent page, and, after a request like the one above, new posts on the Posts page with the badge "Awaiting approval".
Common mistakes
  • Looking for the video's image editor or drag-and-drop calendar in our app. They are not part of our version.
  • Expecting views and likes on the Analytics page. The autopilot does not read them, so neither can we.
  • Trying to connect a social account inside Socialix. Accounts are connected in the autopilot; our Accounts page only reads them.
  • Scheduling an Instagram post without an image URL. It will be refused when you approve it.
Recap
  • The video tours Overview, campaign creation, the AI Agent page, Posts, image editing, Calendar, Accounts and Analytics.
  • Our Socialix has the same pages and the same agent team, with a written campaign brief and an approval step.
  • Not in our version: image generation and editing, cloud desktops, drag and drop, the month view, engagement numbers, the edit/reschedule menu.
