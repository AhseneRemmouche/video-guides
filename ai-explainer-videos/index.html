About the source and the code

Part 1 follows a 10:49 Arabic-language video by the YouTube channel Codezilla and presents its workflow in English for beginners. The two prompts come from the creator's own notes (linked under the video). The video has no code repository, and it does not show how Claude internally builds the video - so Parts 2 and 3 are our own original code, one concrete and reproducible way to make the same kind of explainer. Every example was run and rendered to a real MP4 before being published.
