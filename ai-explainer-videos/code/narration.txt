Have you ever looked up at the sky and seen a giant airplane flying above the clouds?
That airplane is super heavy. Heavier than ten elephants!
So how does something so heavy stay up in the air?
The secret is the wings.
As the airplane speeds down the runway, air rushes over the wings.
The wing is shaped so the air moves faster across the top.
That difference lifts the whole airplane up into the sky.
The engines push it forward, and the wings hold it up.
And that is how airplanes fly!
