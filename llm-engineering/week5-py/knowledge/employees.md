# Insurellm Employee: Maxine Thompson

**Maxine Thompson** is a Senior Data Scientist at Insurellm. She joined the company in 2018 and works out of the Austin, Texas headquarters.

Maxine holds a PhD in Statistics from the University of Washington. She leads the machine learning team responsible for the pricing engine behind the Carllm product.

In 2023, Maxine won Insurellm's internal **"Innovator of the Year" (IIOTY) award** for her work on reducing model inference latency by 40%. Her favorite programming language is Python, and she reports directly to the CEO, Avery Lancaster.
