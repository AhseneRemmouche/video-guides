# About Insurellm

Insurellm is an insurance technology (insurtech) company founded in 2015 by **Avery Lancaster**, who serves as the company's **Chief Executive Officer (CEO)**. The company is headquartered in **Austin, Texas**.

Insurellm started as a disruptor in the insurance market and has since grown to employ over 200 people across four offices in the United States. The company's mission is to make insurance simple, transparent, and affordable for everyone.

Insurellm operates in three main markets: auto insurance, home insurance, and commercial liability. The company serves more than 300 enterprise clients and processes over 1 million policies per year.
