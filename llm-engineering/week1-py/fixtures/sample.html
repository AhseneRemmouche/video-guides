Acme Robotics

Acme Robotics builds friendly warehouse robots that help small businesses pack and ship orders faster. Founded in 2019, we are a team of 40 engineers and designers based in Portland, Oregon.

What we do

Our flagship robot, the PackBot One, can sort up to 2,000 parcels an hour. Customers include regional grocers, craft marketplaces, and a growing number of online toy stores.

Latest news

  • October 2026: We closed a $12M Series A to expand into Canada.
  • September 2026: PackBot One won the Northwest Automation Award.

Culture

We value curiosity, kindness, and shipping small improvements every week. Everyone gets Fridays for learning and side projects.
