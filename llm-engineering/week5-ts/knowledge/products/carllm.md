# Product: Carllm

Carllm is Insurellm's flagship product: a car insurance pricing and quoting
platform sold to insurance carriers. It was first released in 2016 and is now
used by more than 30 carriers in the United States.

## What it does

- Real-time quoting: carriers get an instant premium for a driver and vehicle.
- Risk scoring: a machine-learning model estimates the risk of each policy.
- A/B pricing experiments so carriers can tune rates by region.

## Pricing

Carllm is sold as a subscription with three tiers:

- **Basic** - $1,000 per month, up to 1,000 quotes per day.
- **Professional** - $2,500 per month, up to 10,000 quotes per day.
- **Enterprise** - custom pricing, unlimited quotes and a dedicated success manager.

All tiers include standard support; Enterprise adds a 99.9% uptime guarantee.
