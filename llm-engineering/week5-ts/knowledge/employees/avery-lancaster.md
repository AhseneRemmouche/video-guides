# Employee: Avery Lancaster

- **Title:** Co-founder and Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
- **Joined:** 2015 (founding)
- **Location:** San Francisco

## Background

Avery Lancaster founded Insurellm in 2015 after a decade working in actuarial
and product roles at large insurance carriers. Avery studied mathematics at the
University of Manchester before moving into the insurance industry.

## Recognition

Avery won the company's **Insurellm Innovator of the Year (IIOTY)** award in
2023, recognising the launch of the Markellm marketplace. Under Avery's
leadership Insurellm grew from a single product to a four-product suite serving
hundreds of carriers.

Avery is known internally for a hands-on management style and for championing
the "insurance should be understandable" principle that guides the company's
product design.
