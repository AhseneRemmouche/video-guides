# About Insurellm

Insurellm is an insurance technology ("insurtech") startup founded in 2015 by
Avery Lancaster. The company builds software that helps traditional insurance
carriers modernise their products and reach customers online.

Insurellm grew quickly from a single product to a suite of four platforms, and
today employs around 200 people across the United States. The company is
headquartered in San Francisco, with regional offices in Austin and Denver.

## Products at a glance

- **Carllm** - a car insurance pricing and quoting platform for carriers.
- **Homellm** - a home insurance portal.
- **Rellm** - a reinsurance analytics suite.
- **Markellm** - a two-sided marketplace connecting consumers with insurers.

Insurellm's mission is to make insurance faster, fairer and easier to
understand, both for the carriers who sell it and the customers who buy it.
