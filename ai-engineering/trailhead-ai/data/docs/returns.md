# Returns and refunds

You can return any unused item within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Bikes must have fewer than 50 km on the odometer and no scratches on the frame.

Opened helmets cannot be returned for hygiene reasons, unless they arrived damaged.

To start a return, open your order in your account and choose "Return an item". We email a prepaid return label within one business day. Return shipping is free for orders over 100 EUR; for smaller orders we deduct 6.90 EUR from the refund.

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days after the parcel reaches our warehouse in Lyon.
