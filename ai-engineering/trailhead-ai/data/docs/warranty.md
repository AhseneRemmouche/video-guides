# Warranty

Every Trailhead frame has a lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects for the original owner. Components (brakes, gears, wheels, saddles) are covered for 2 years.

E-bike batteries and motors are covered for 2 years or 500 full charge cycles, whichever comes first.

The warranty does not cover normal wear (tyres, brake pads, chains), crash damage, or bikes used for rental or competition.

To make a claim, send a photo of the problem, your order number and the frame number (stamped under the bottom bracket) to warranty@trailhead.example. We answer within 3 business days.
