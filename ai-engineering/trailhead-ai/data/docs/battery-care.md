# E-bike battery care

Charge the battery at room temperature, between 10 and 30 degrees Celsius. Never charge a battery that is below 0 degrees; let it warm up indoors first.

For storage longer than a month, keep the battery at 40 to 60 percent charge and check it every 8 weeks.

A full charge from empty takes about 4.5 hours with the standard 4 A charger, or 2.5 hours with the fast charger.

Range depends on assist level, weight and terrain: expect 60 to 110 km per charge on the Trailhead Volt 500 Wh battery.

If the battery light blinks red three times, the battery is too cold or too hot to charge. Do not open the battery case; this voids the warranty.
