# Stores and workshops

Trailhead has three stores with workshops: Lyon (12 rue de la Charite), Grenoble (4 place Victor Hugo) and Annecy (27 avenue de Geneve).

All stores are open Tuesday to Saturday, 10 am to 7 pm. They are closed on Sunday and Monday.

Workshop appointments can be booked online. A standard service takes about 2 hours; a full service takes a full day, so drop the bike off before 10 am.

Customer support answers by chat and email Monday to Friday, 9 am to 6 pm (Paris time), at support@trailhead.example.
