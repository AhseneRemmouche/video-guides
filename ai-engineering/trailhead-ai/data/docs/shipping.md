# Shipping

We ship to France, Belgium, Germany, Spain, Italy and the Netherlands.

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and costs 9.90 EUR. It is free on orders over 100 EUR.

Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 19.90 EUR. Express is not available for complete bikes because of their size.

Complete bikes arrive 90% assembled in a box; you only attach the handlebar, pedals and front wheel. Orders placed before 2 pm (Paris time) on a business day leave the warehouse the same day.
