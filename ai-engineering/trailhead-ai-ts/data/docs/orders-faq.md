# Orders and payment

We accept credit and debit cards, PayPal and bank transfer. Bank transfer orders are shipped once the money arrives, usually after 1 to 2 business days.

You can pay for bikes over 1000 EUR in 3 or 4 interest-free instalments.

You can change or cancel an order until it leaves the warehouse. After that, use the normal return process.

Every order has a number that looks like TH-10234. You find it in the confirmation email and in your account.
