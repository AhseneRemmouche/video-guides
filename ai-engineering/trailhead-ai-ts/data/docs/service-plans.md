# Service plans

Trailhead Care is a yearly service plan for 89 EUR. It includes one full workshop service per year, free brake and gear adjustments at any time, and 15 percent off parts.

Trailhead Care Plus costs 149 EUR per year. It adds theft protection up to the bike's purchase price for bikes locked with an approved lock, and free pick-up and delivery for workshop visits within 20 km of a Trailhead store.

Service plans can be bought within 60 days of buying a bike and renew automatically every year. You can cancel at any time from your account.
