  • Skim every week's recap box across both guides.

Days 29–30 are your safety margin — if nothing slipped, use them to rest or go deeper on a framework you liked.

Tips to stay on track

  • One rest day a week is built in. The content needs ~26 study days; you have 30, so take a day off when you need it — the buffer absorbs it.
  • If a day slips, don't skip the build — slip the build. Keep watching + reading on schedule and move the missed capstone to Days 29–30. Falling behind on watching is what derails the month, not a late build.
  • GPU weeks (LLM W3, W7) use free Google Colab. The frontier fine-tune (LLM W6), parts of W8 (Modal deploy), and the agentic trading floor need paid keys for the full run — follow each build page's RUN-NOTES and use the free local Ollama path where offered.
  • Stay in one language (Python). It covers all 14 weeks. Only the bilingual weeks (every LLM week; Agentic W1/W2/W4) have a TypeScript port — save those for a second pass.
  • Protect the last four days (25–28). Weeks 8 (LLM) and 6 (Agentic) are the payoff projects — guard that time even if you trim a lab earlier.
